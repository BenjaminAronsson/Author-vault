# arkiv-C09 — Husförhörslängd Motala stad 1866–1880, Erik Svensson

## Summary
Husförhörslängd for Motala stad 1866–1880 for the household of the smith Erik Svensson. It gives Erik (born 1839-09-30 in Motala, died 1880-04-12) and his wife Anna Persdotter (born 1843-05-12 in Kila, moved in 1866 from Kila, married 1866-10-21), and three children born in Motala: Karl 1867, Maria 1870 and Hilda 1873 (died 1874-02-03). The birth date for Anna agrees with the records and differs from the family tradition of midsommar 1844. Erik's death in 1880 is new. The wedding date 1866-10-21 supports the earlier 1866 marriage. Facts without their own date are only covered by the span of the book. Anna and Erik are taken to be the already known persons (matching name, spouse, birth date, Kila).
