# Saltmarrow

## Overview
A salt-marsh village where the river meets the sea, ruled by a reeve and haunted by the flood of forty years ago.

## Tone & texture
Wet wool, tar smoke, gulls. "The river keeps what you give it." ([[B1-C01]])

## Rules (magic / tech / society)
- **Technology level:** watermills, tar boats, iron tools. No gunpowder, no printing.
- Salt-singing works only at the tide line. ([[Salt-Singing]])
